# ADR 002: Responsible nutrition matching

Status: Accepted for V2 demo

## Decision

Use a conservative, versioned produce taxonomy to match inventory with five general nutrition objectives. Show supporting produce and short nutrient attributes, cite recognized sources, and always display a general-education disclaimer. Use “supports” language.

## Why

DRN should help people discover food without collecting diagnoses or presenting itself as a medical-advice system. Model-assisted normalization may suggest tags, but published mappings must come from an approved taxonomy and be reviewable by community health partners.

## Consequences

The taxonomy is intentionally incomplete. It must not generate treatment advice, contraindication advice, or claims that a food prevents or cures disease. Live expansion requires source review and partner validation.
